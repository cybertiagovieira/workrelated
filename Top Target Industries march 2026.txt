**Key Insights & Anomalies for March 2026:**
*   **Manufacturing** remained the definitive top target globally, seeing a massive surge of 42 additional attacks in the Comparitech dataset alone compared to the previous month. 
*   **Utilities** experienced an exponential 630% spike in attacks (rising from 3 in February to 22 in March). This is largely driven by state-aligned or hacktivist threat actors attempting to inflict maximum societal disruption and impact critical infrastructure.
*   **Healthcare** targeting showed some signs of cooling off from its previous highs, dropping from 93 victims in February to 47 in March according to Breachsense. Despite this drop, the industry remains highly exposed and regularly targeted.
