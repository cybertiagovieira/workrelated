### Executive Cybersecurity Report: Monthly Trends Analysis

**Phishing**
**Real-time vishing** attacks are increasingly bypassing **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**, with distinct campaigns currently targeting **motor carriers** and the **logistics** sector. Attackers are heavily exploiting **Microsoft Teams** for credential harvesting, particularly in **Brazil**, while **Google Presentations** is being abused to host sophisticated phishing landing pages to evade standard detection filters.

**Ransomware**
The **ShinyHunters** group remains highly active, driving a strategic shift toward **extortion-only** attacks that bypass encryption to enforce payment leverage despite the availability of decryptors. Dark web intelligence indicates a surge in high-value data theft operations, with significant chatter linking recent activity to large-scale compromises in the **entertainment** and **financial** sectors.

**Banking Trojans**
The **BlackMoon** trojan is aggressively targeting **Indian** taxpayers via espionage-focused phishing, while the **Astaroth (Boto Cor de Rosa)** worm has re-emerged in **Brazil** using **WhatsApp** for rapid propagation. Additionally, the new **Albiriox** Android RAT has been identified, capable of full **device takeover** and real-time control of victim endpoints.

**Carding**
Underground markets are seeing a high volume of trade in **"Fullz"** (full credential sets) and **CVV** data, sustained by specialized carding communities. Threat actors are currently prioritizing the exploitation of **e-commerce checkout** vulnerabilities and physical **skimmers** to harvest payment data at scale from retail environments.

**Denial of Service**
The **Aisuru** botnet established a new world record with a massive **31.4 Tbps** DDoS attack, severely impacting **gaming servers** and health data hubs. Critical vulnerabilities in **bind**, **glibc**, and **CoreDNS** (CVE-2025/2026 series) are being actively weaponized as amplification vectors to launch sustained stress tests against financial infrastructure.

**BEC Attacks**
**Adversary-in-the-Middle (AiTM)** campaigns are weaponizing **Microsoft SharePoint** to steal session tokens, contributing to a **15% increase** in attacks specifically targeting the **energy sector**. While authorities successfully dismantled the **RedVdS** fraud engine, **AI-driven** voice cloning deepfakes are increasingly being utilized to authorize fraudulent high-value payments.

**Account Takeover**
The **ZombieAgent** zero-click vulnerability and critical flaws in **Appsmith (CVE-2026-22794)** have exposed millions of users to silent account takeover. Malicious **Chrome extensions** are actively harvesting API keys from **MEXC** exchange users, prompting defense firm **Memcyco** to raise **$37M** to combat this rise in digital impersonation.

**Data Breach**
**Coupang's** interim CEO faces police questioning over a major breach, while **Match Group** (Hinge) and **Canada Computers** have confirmed significant customer data leaks. In the public sector, the **US Government** faces a **$10B** lawsuit regarding IRS tax data leaks, and fintech firm **Marquis** has attributed their compromise to a **SonicWall** firewall vulnerability.

**Third Party Risk**
Supply chain vulnerabilities in **SonicWall** devices are being actively exploited to breach downstream clients, highlighting that **software integrity** now critically outweighs data sovereignty. **Black Kite** has released "ThreatTrace" to track these cascading risks, while market volatility in **ServiceNow** and **CrowdStrike** stocks reflects growing anxiety over vendor security postures.

**Attacks on Digital Assets**
The **US DOJ** finalized a **$400M** forfeiture from the **Helix** darknet mixer, while the **US Marshals Service** is investigating a potential internal hack of government-controlled digital assets. Simultaneously, **Ethereum** developers are repurposing unclaimed funds from the historic 2016 **The DAO** hack to establish a new ecosystem security fund.
