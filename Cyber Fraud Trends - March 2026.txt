*   **Geopolitically-Driven Financial Fraud in the UAE:** Following late-February military escalations, March 2026 saw Iranian state-aligned actors deploy "Emirates-focused" and "Dubai-themed" fraud campaigns, mimicking UAE government entities, real estate assets, and Emirates Post to harvest credit card data and cryptocurrency.
*   **Post-Breach Phishing Escalation in France:** Following the late-February disclosure of the FICOBA registry breach, March 2026 was characterized by a targeted "phishing frenzy" utilizing verified tax identifiers and IBANs tied to 1.2 million French accounts to facilitate synthetic identity fraud and direct debit scams.

*   **Telecommunications Billing Fraud via Subdomain Chaining:** Intelligence identified an active March campaign impersonating a major regional telecommunications provider by utilizing fake dialing-code prefixes and rotating top-level domains (TLDs) to bypass blocking mechanisms and execute consumer billing fraud.
*   **StealC Infostealer Infrastructure Evasion:** Monitoring of credential theft infrastructure in March 2026 revealed StealC operators adopting a numbered-increment pattern across identical TLDs to rapidly evade domain blocking and sustain the supply of compromised credentials to downstream fraud operators.
